dusting off the old property path experiment results.
I did not re-run the experiments - hoping we can make do with these ones.

terminology:
foward-chaining generated rules: "fwd"
backward-chaining generated rules: "bwd"
direct rules (no pre-generation): "direct"


(1) fwd does better on gmark.
mean performance (gmark-50) -
fwd: 103ms
jena: 202ms
bwd: 1,770ms
direct: 5,283ms

mean performance (gmark-100) -
fwd: 326ms
jena: 1,687ms
(did not run bwd or direct)


(2) jena does much better on zika.
mean performance (5000 patients, 2% chance of matching condition) -
jena: 207ms
bwd: 6,578ms
fwd: 28,260ms
direct: 59,101ms

mean performance (10000 patients, 2% chance of matching condition) -
jena: 276ms
bwd: 22,998ms
fwd: 86,266ms
direct: 31,908ms (no idea why it takes less long here)

sin3 did quite badly for 2 of the rules here (pp2 and pp4).


(3) to improve the zika performance, we tried bwd with manual re-ordering of clauses ("bwd-ro") for these 2 rules. this improved performance a lot.
5000 -
jena: pp2: 209ms, pp4: 194ms
bwd-ro: pp2: 2209ms, pp4: 1333ms

10000 -
jena: pp2: 286ms, pp4: 256ms
bwd-ro: pp2: 6,660ms, pp4: 3,822ms


(4) then, we tried making the rules more restrictive. as expected, this improved performance by much more.

the most restrictive version (only single patient):
10000 -
jena: pp2: 82ms, pp4: 83ms
bwd-ro: 62ms, pp4: 58ms

a less restrictive version (restricting to a type of resource):
10000 -
jena: pp2: 121ms, pp4: 186ms
bwd-ro: pp2: 344ms, pp4: 1,652ms

so, clause re-ordering and restrictions are a potential solution for the zika dataset.

some observations -
sin3 does better for the gmark "50" and "100" datasets, but these are tiny datasets (max. 125 lines). 
on smaller zika datasets, sin3 does not do too badly:
- for 1000 patients (36k lines), 119ms for jena, 370ms for bwd, 1,536ms for fwd, 1,386ms for direct
- for 2000 patients (73k lines), this gap starts to widen - 144ms for jena, 1,150ms for bwd, 4,355ms for fwd, 5,412ms for direct
it clearly goes downhill from there. a way to fix this is to simply have "sane" queries that put restrictions on the variables in the property paths.

unsure why fwd is much better on gmark and worse on zika though.
